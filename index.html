Peacock TV Troubleshooting Guide

If Peacock TV is not working correctly, several basic troubleshooting steps can often help identify and resolve common problems.

Tip: Before troubleshooting, make sure your device has a stable internet connection and that your Peacock app is updated to the latest available version.

1. Check Your Internet Connection

A slow or unstable connection can cause buffering, playback interruptions, or difficulty loading content.

  • Check whether other websites or streaming services work.
  • Restart your Wi-Fi router if necessary.
  • Move your device closer to your Wi-Fi router.
  • Try another available network when possible.

2. Restart the Peacock App

Completely close the Peacock application and open it again. On mobile devices and smart TVs, you may need to exit the application before reopening it.

3. Restart Your Device

Restarting your television, streaming device, computer, tablet, or smartphone can clear temporary software issues.

4. Update the Application

Make sure you are using the latest version of the Peacock application available for your device. Older versions may experience compatibility or playback issues.

5. Check Your Account

If you cannot access certain content or features, verify that you are signed in to the correct account and that your subscription status is active.

6. Clear Temporary Data

Depending on your device, clearing the application's cache or reinstalling the app may resolve persistent loading or playback problems.

7. Contact Official Support

If the problem continues after trying the basic troubleshooting steps, use Peacock's official help and support resources for account-specific assistance.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common Peacock TV troubleshooting questions.

Why is Peacock TV buffering?

Buffering can be caused by an unstable or slow internet connection, network congestion, device issues, or temporary service problems.

Why won't Peacock TV open?

Try closing and reopening the app, restarting your device, checking your internet connection, and installing available application updates.

Why can't I sign in?

Verify your login information and make sure you are using the correct Peacock account. For account-specific problems, consult Peacock's official support resources.

What should I do if videos won't play?

Check your internet connection, restart the app and device, and make sure the application is updated.

Conclusion

Peacock TV streaming problems can often be resolved by following a few simple troubleshooting steps. Checking your internet connection, restarting the application, restarting your device, and keeping the app updated are useful first steps.

If an issue continues after completing these steps, the problem may require account-specific or service-specific assistance. In that situation, it is recommended to use Peacock's official support resources for further help.

Keeping your device, application, and network connection in good working condition can help provide a smoother streaming experience.
